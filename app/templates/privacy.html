What we collect

We collect the minimum needed to operate the Service:

  • The documents you upload, for the duration of processing.
  • The settings you choose for each operation.
  • Standard web-server logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) for security and abuse prevention.

How documents are handled

Uploaded documents are stored in a private temporary directory on our servers, inaccessible over the public web. They are automatically deleted according to our retention policy — typically within {{ retention_seconds // 60 }} minutes of job completion. We do not read the contents of your documents, and the file contents and passwords are never written to logs.

Third parties

Documents are processed entirely on our own infrastructure. We do not send documents to any external conversion or AI provider.

Cookies

We use a single session cookie for CSRF protection. It is signed and does not contain any personal information. No third-party analytics or advertising cookies are set.

Reporting a concern

If you believe your data has been handled improperly, contact the maintainers via the project repository.
