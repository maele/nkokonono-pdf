Licensing

Nkokonono ea PDF is open source. This page describes the licence on our code, the licences of the libraries and tools we depend on, and what you should know before redistributing or hosting the project.

This page is informational, not legal advice. If you plan to redistribute or host Nkokonono commercially, consult a lawyer about how the licence mix below applies to your situation.

Important: copyleft dependencies

Three dependencies have strong copyleft licences:

  • PyMuPDF (AGPL-3.0) — used by the PDF-to-images and PDF-to-PowerPoint tools, and by page previews in the Organise and Merge tools.
  • pdf2docx (GPL-3.0) — used by the PDF-to-Word tool.
  • Ghostscript (AGPL-3.0) — used by OCRmyPDF on the server, for the OCR tool.

What this means in practice:

  • Running Nkokonono privately, for yourself or your team, does not trigger copyleft obligations.
  • Redistributing it publicly (e.g. publishing a fork, packaging for a distro) requires you to comply with AGPL-3.0 and GPL-3.0 — typically by offering source code to recipients under the same licence.
  • Hosting it as a public service: AGPL-3.0 §13 requires offering source code to the users of the service for the AGPL-licensed parts. How this applies to your own surrounding code is a matter for legal advice.
  • Commercial hosting often resolves this by obtaining a commercial licence from the vendors of the affected tools, or by removing those tools from their deployment.

We make no representation about your compliance. If you plan to deploy Nkokonono publicly, review the AGPL-3.0, GPL-3.0, and MPL-2.0 obligations, or replace the affected tools with permissively-licensed alternatives before you ship.

Verifying the licences yourself

Every dependency's licence is listed in its own distribution. To see exactly what's installed in your environment: 

pip install pip-licenses
pip-licenses --format=markdown --with-urls

For system binaries (LibreOffice, Tesseract, Ghostscript), check the licence files distributed with the installed packages or on their upstream project pages.
